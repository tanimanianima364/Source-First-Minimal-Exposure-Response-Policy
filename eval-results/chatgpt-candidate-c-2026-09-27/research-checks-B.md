## Pilot uncertainty limitation

§5 proposes24 tasks, repeated seeds on8 tasks, blinded experts, task-level bootstrap and prospective gates. However, it uses a bootstrap95% upper bound below +2pp for rare major errors without a zero-event safeguard. With zero major errors in both arms on all24 tasks, an ordinary empirical paired bootstrap produces only zero risk differences and can pass that safety gate. It does not establish that a +2pp population harm is excluded. For illustration, a10% per-task harmful-event probability still has .9^24≈7.98% chance of zero observed events. This is a counterexample to the proposed uncertainty procedure, not an estimate of either policy's error rate. Small-sample/zero-event inference or an explicit inconclusive gate is needed before treating the pilot as safety evidence. The answer acknowledges limited power but does not supply that safeguard.
